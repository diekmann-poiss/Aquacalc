FAO-56 Species and Site-Specific Water Demand Calculator with Vertical Greening

About

AquaCalc calculates irrigation water requirements using the FAO-56 Penman-Monteith method. It provides planning estimates for landscape, urban greening, and agricultural projects.

Climate data is sourced from Open-Meteo API (ERA5 reanalysis) with selectable time periods (5, 10, or 30 years).

Citation

Poiss, M. (2026) Aquacalc (Version 2.7) [Computer software]. Available at: https://diekmann-poiss.github.io/Aquacalc/ (Accessed: DAY MONTH YEAR).

Horizontal Planting

Method: ETc = ET₀ × Kc × situation demand factor

Effective rain: Rainfall × 0.8 × situation rain factor

Net irrigation: ETc − Effective Rain (never below zero)

Unit conversion: 1 mm over 1 m² = 1 litre

Situation Factors

  • Open: Demand: 1.0, Rain: 1.0
  • Against a Wall: Demand: 1.15, Rain: 0.75
  • Under an Overhang: Demand: 1.0, Rain: 0.3
  • Roofed: Demand: 0.9, Rain: 0.0
  • Shaded/North: Demand: 0.7, Rain: 0.9

Vertical Greening

Extends FAO-56 to vertical surfaces with monthly-resolved calculations for solar geometry, plant phenology, and microclimate effects.

Core equation: V_irr(m) = max(0, ETc_vert(m) − P_eff(m))

Key Parameters

  • Facade Orientation: Dynamic monthly factors for solar radiation
  • System Factor (Kf): Soil-based: 0.9, Trough: 1.0, Living Wall: 1.15-1.3
  • Shading: None: 1.0, Light: 0.75, Moderate: 0.5, Heavy: 0.25
  • Ground Albedo: Asphalt: 0.15, Concrete: 0.30, Grass: 0.20-0.25
  • Effective Rain: ~20% of rainfall reaches vertical surfaces
  • Crop Coefficients: Monthly profiles for 35 climbing plants

Vertical Greening Formulas

Verticalized Solar Radiation:

ET0,vert(m) = ET0(m) × Forient(m) × Fshading + ET0(m) × Falbedo × (1 - Fshading)

Where:

  • Forient(m): Monthly orientation factor based on solar geometry (N=0.3, NE=0.5, E=0.7, SE=0.9, S=1.0, SW=0.9, W=0.7, NW=0.5)
  • Fshading: Direct radiation reduction factor from opposite buildings (None=1.0, Light=0.75, Medium=0.5, Heavy=0.25)
  • Falbedo: Ground cover albedo (Asphalt=0.15, Concrete=0.30, Grass=0.20-0.25)

Effective Precipitation for Vertical Surfaces:

Peff(m) = P(m) × 0.2 × (1 - Froof)

Where: Froof = 0.3 if roof overhang present, else 0

Vertical Crop Water Demand:

ETc,vert(m) = ET0,vert(m) × Kc(m) × Kf

Where: Kf = System factor (Soil-based: 0.9, Trough: 1.0, Living Wall: 1.15-1.3)
