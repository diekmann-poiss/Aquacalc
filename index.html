FAO-56 Species and Site-Specific Water Demand Calculator

About

AquaCalc calculates irrigation water requirements using the FAO-56 Penman-Monteith method. It provides planning estimates for landscape and urban greening projects.

How It Works

Crop water use: ETc = ET₀ × Kc × situation demand factor

Effective rain: Rainfall × 0.8 × situation rain factor

Net irrigation: ETc - Effective Rain (never below zero)

Data Sources

  • Primary: FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 56 (Allen et al., 1998)
  • ET₀ data: FAO CLIMWAT (global cross-check), GeoSphere Austria, Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD)
  • Kc values: FAO-56, WUCOLS, Doorenbos & Pruitt (1977)

Note on Climate Data

City climate values are planning estimates following typical regional patterns. They are not official measured data. For precise data, connect to Open-Meteo API.
