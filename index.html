AquaCalc

FAO-56 Species and Site-Specific Water Demand Calculator

About

AquaCalc calculates irrigation water requirements using the FAO-56 Penman-Monteith method. It provides planning estimates for landscape and urban greening projects, as well as agricultural crops.

How It Works

Crop water use: ETc = ET₀ × Kc × situation demand factor

Effective rain: Rainfall × 0.8 × situation rain factor

Net irrigation: ETc - Effective Rain (never below zero)

Unit conversion: 1 mm over 1 m² = 1 litre

Demand and Rain Factors

  • Open: Demand: 1.0, Rain: 1.0
  • Against a Wall: Demand: 1.15, Rain: 0.75 (increased heat reflection)
  • Under an Overhang: Demand: 1.0, Rain: 0.3 (reduced rainfall)
  • Roofed: Demand: 0.9, Rain: 0.0 (no rainfall)
  • Shaded / North Side: Demand: 0.7, Rain: 0.9 (reduced evapotranspiration)

Data Sources

Methodology:

  • FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 56 (Allen et al., 1998)
  • ASCE-EWRI standardized reference ET equation (2005)
  • WUCOLS landscape water-use classification (Costello et al., UC Cooperative Extension)
  • Doorenbos & Pruitt (1977)

ET₀ Data:

  • GeoSphere Austria
  • Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) Climate Data Center
  • MeteoSwiss
  • FAO CLIMWAT (global cross-check)
  • Open-Meteo API (for live data when enabled)

Kc Values: FAO-56, WUCOLS, and regionally adapted values for landscape plants.

Note on Climate Data

City climate values are planning estimates following typical regional patterns. They are not official measured data. For precise data, enable the Open-Meteo API option in the Site tab.

Irrigation Types

  • Drip Line: 5 days/week, flow rate: 1.6-2.4 L/min/emitter
  • Soaker Hose: 4 days/week, flow rate: 2.5-3.5 L/min/m
  • Micro-Sprayer: 3 days/week, flow rate: 40-60 L/h/sprayer
  • Pop-up Sprinkler: 2 days/week, flow rate: 100-140 L/h/sprinkler
